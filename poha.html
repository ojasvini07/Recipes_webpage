POHA

Poha, a popular Indian flattened rice dish originating from Maharashtra, is a nutritious, low-calorie, and quick-cooking breakfast or snack. It is made from flattened rice, turmeric, often garnished with lemon and peanuts. It's high in iron and fiber.

Ingredients

  • Flattened Rice
  • Onions
  • Peanuts
  • Tempered spices
  • Lemon juice

Procedure

  1. Wash and Soak the Poha
  2. Fry Peanuts and Potatoes
  3. Prepare the Tempering (Tadka)
  4. Sauté Onions and Spices
  5. Mix Everything
  6. Steam the Poha
  7. Garnish and Serve
