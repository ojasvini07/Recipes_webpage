PAV BHAJI

Pav Bhaji is a iconic Indian street food consisting of a thick, spicy vegetable curry (bhaji) served hot with soft, buttered bread rolls (pav). Topped with fresh coriander, chopped onions, and a dollop of butter, it is a hearty and irresistible meal.

Ingredients

  • Potatoes, green peas, and cauliflower
  • Tomatoes and onions
  • Pav bhaji masala
  • Butter and oil
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Fresh cilantro and lemon
  • Pav buns

Procedure

  1. Boil and mash the vegetables (potatoes, peas, cauliflower).
  2. Sauté onions, ginger, and garlic in butter.
  3. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until soft.
  4. Mix in pav bhaji masala, red chili powder, and salt.
  5. Add mashed veggies, water, and simmer for 10 minutes.
  6. Toast pav buns on a griddle with generous butter.
  7. Garnish with cilantro, butter, and lemon juice; serve hot.
