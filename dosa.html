MASALA DOSA

Masala Dosa is a traditional South Indian staple featuring a thin, crispy fermented rice and urad dal crepe folded over a savory, spiced potato filling. It is traditionally served alongside fresh coconut chutney and flavorful hot sambar.

Ingredients

  • Fermented Dosa batter
  • Boiled potatoes
  • Onions and green chilies
  • Mustard seeds and curry leaves
  • Turmeric powder
  • Oil or ghee
  • Coconut chutney & Sambar (for serving)

Procedure

  1. Heat oil and temper mustard seeds, curry leaves, and chilies.
  2. Sauté onions and turmeric powder until translucent.
  3. Add crumbled boiled potatoes and salt; mix well to make potato masala.
  4. Pour a ladle of batter on a hot tawa and spread in circles.
  5. Drizzle oil or ghee around the edges until golden and crisp.
  6. Place a portion of potato masala in the center and fold.
  7. Serve crisp and hot with chutney and sambar.
