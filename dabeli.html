DABELI

Dabeli, or Kutchi Dabeli, is a popular sweet, spicy, and tangy Indian street food snack originating from the Kutch region of Gujarat. It consists of a savory potato mixture stuffed inside a burger-like bun, topped with roasted peanuts, pomegranate seeds, sev, and vibrant chutneys.

Ingredients

  • Boiled potatoes
  • Garlic chutney
  • Tamarind chutney
  • Specialized Dabeli masala
  • Roasted peanuts
  • Pomegranate seeds
  • Butter
  • Pav

Procedure

  1. Prepare the Stuffing
  2. Add Toppings to Mixture
  3. Prepare the Pav
  4. Apply Chutneys
  5. Assemble the Dabeli
  6. Toast the Dabeli
  7. Final Garnish and Serve
