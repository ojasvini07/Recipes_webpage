PIZZA

Pizza is a popular Italian dish consisting of a flattened, leavened wheat-based dough crust, traditionally topped with tomato sauce and mozzarella cheese. Baked at high temperatures, it has evolved into a global favorite with countless custom topping combinations.

Ingredients

  • Simple dough (flour, yeast, water, salt, olive oil)
  • Flavorful tomato-based sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Choice of toppings (vegetables, meats, herbs)

Procedure

  1. Make the Dough
  2. Prepare the Sauce and Toppings
  3. Shape the Dough
  4. Assemble the Pizza
  5. Bake the Pizza
  6. Slice and Serve
