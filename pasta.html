CREAMY PASTA

Creamy White Sauce Pasta is a delicious Italian favorite made with boiled penne pasta tossed in a smooth, velvety garlic-parmesan sauce along with colorful sautéed bell peppers and Italian seasonings.

Ingredients

  • Penne or your choice of pasta
  • Butter and all-purpose flour (for roux)
  • Milk
  • Minced garlic
  • Bell peppers and sweet corn
  • Grated cheese (Mozzarella/Parmesan)
  • Oregano and red chili flakes

Procedure

  1. Boil pasta in salted water until al dente, then drain.
  2. Sauté garlic and veggies in a little butter, then set aside.
  3. Melt butter, add flour, and whisk for 1 minute to form a roux.
  4. Gradually pour in milk while whisking to avoid lumps.
  5. Simmer sauce until thick; stir in seasonings and cheese.
  6. Add cooked pasta and sautéed veggies to the sauce.
  7. Toss well and serve warm with garlic bread.
